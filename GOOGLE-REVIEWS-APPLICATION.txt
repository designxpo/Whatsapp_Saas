TENANT SETUP
Requirements: Creator Pro, Growth or Scale; a verified Google Business Profile; sign in as that profile's owner or manager.
1. Open Google Reviews in Talko.
2. Select Connect with Google, choose the managing account and approve access in the same browser session.
3. Select the business location.
4. Open Settings and choose reply automation, signature and tone.
5. New Google reviews are imported by the scheduled worker. By default, 4–5 star replies auto-post and 1–3 star replies wait for approval. Choose Never auto-post to approve every reply.
6. Review a draft, edit it if needed and choose Post to Google.
7. Use Disconnect to stop importing new reviews and posting through that connection.
For a manual pasted review, Copy the reply and post it on Google yourself; Mark posted only records that manual action in Talko.
