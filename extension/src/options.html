How this stays safe. The extension only ever talks to your own Talko backend over HTTPS — never to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or any other site directly. It reads a page only when you ask it to: the text you highlight, or the contact details it finds when you press Scan page. Results are proposed, never saved on their own — you tick each row first — and every lead it adds is recorded in your portal with its source. Sending still goes through the official WhatsApp Cloud API with your approved templates and opt-out rules.
